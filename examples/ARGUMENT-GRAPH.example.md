# ARGUMENT-GRAPH — does a shorter trial convert better?

## Questions
- Q1 Does a 7-day trial convert better than a 14-day trial for this product?

## Claims
- C1 Shorter trials create urgency and raise conversion. (by: agent)
- C2 Products with a slow time-to-value lose conversions on short trials. (by: user)

## Evidence
- E1 supports C1: vendor benchmark report, one source, self-published. Weak.
- E2 supports C2: our own onboarding data, median time to first value is 9 days. Strong.

## Findings
- F1 For this product, C2 dominates while time to first value exceeds 7 days.

## Objections
- O1 to F1: the 9-day median may be an artefact of the current onboarding emails.

## Open questions
- Q2 What is time to first value after the new onboarding sequence ships?
